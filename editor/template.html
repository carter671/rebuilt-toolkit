    How to

    Click a wall to remove it or add a door or window.
    Several walls: Shift-click (or Ctrl-click) each one, or use Box select and drag around them. Then drag any of them to move them together, or remove them all at once.
    Drag a wall to move it. The walls attached to it stretch so everything stays connected. To move just that one wall, turn on Move one wall only or hold Alt while you drag.
    Drag the round handles on a selected wall to make it longer or shorter.
    Add room: click one corner, then the opposite corner. It snaps to existing walls and reuses any wall already there. Outside the house it becomes an addition with outside walls that match the building it's attached to.
    Add door / Add window: click the button, then click the wall where it goes. Click the new door to change its size or drag it along the wall.
    Draw wall: click where it starts and ends. Hold Shift for any angle.
    Click a door or window to resize or remove it; drag it to slide it along the wall.
    Arrow keys nudge what's selected 1" (Shift 12", Alt ¼"). Hold Shift while placing a cabinet to place it without connecting.
    Cabinets & furniture: pick an item under Add to the plan, move to a wall and click. It backs up to the wall and snaps to its neighbours. Click a wall and choose Cabinet run to fill it with base and upper cabinets.
    Roof framing: set each roof to trusses or rafters once you know. Walls outlined in amber may hold up the ceiling; removing one asks for a beam. Show framing draws the trusses in 3D.
    Click a room to rename it. Scroll to zoom; drag empty space to pan.
